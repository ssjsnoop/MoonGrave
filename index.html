How to play

Goal: survive 100 waves across five lands: Greenfield Meadow (1–20), Hollow Moor (21–40), Whispering Woods (41–60), Frostpeak (61–80) and the brutal Ashen Citadel (81–100). In the Citadel foes sometimes encircle you with a gap in the ring: follow the gold arrow and run for it. A boss waits on every 5th wave (5, 10, 15…) and a smaller sub-boss on waves 3, 8, 13…. Every wave brings more foes, and they grow tougher every few waves. Kill foes to drop green orbs — they drift to you. Level up to earn skill points, then pause to spend them on Sword, Fireball, Energy Blast, or bonuses. Upgrades never cap.

Touch: left thumb = move · ⚔ sword (hold to repeat) · 🔥 fireball · ⚡ hold to charge a bigger blast, release to fire. Attacks aim at the nearest enemy.
Keyboard + mouse: WASD/arrows move · J/Space or left-click sword · K or right-click fire · hold L/E (or middle-click) energy · mouse aims · P/Esc pause.
Controller: left stick move · right stick aim · A sword · X fire · hold B or RT energy · Start pause · D-pad/A in menus.

Gear: foes sometimes drop glowing weapons and pets — walk over them. You can carry 5. They attack on their own; grabbing a duplicate levels it up (max 10). Pause to drop one. Bosses drop two. Sword upgrades also stretch its reach.

The sword can swing while you charge. Charge longer = bigger and stronger; a full charge fills a quarter of the screen and flies for 10 seconds.
