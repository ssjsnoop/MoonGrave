How to play

Goal: survive 100 waves across five lands: Greenfield Meadow (1–20), Hollow Moor (21–40), Whispering Woods (41–60), Frostpeak (61–80) and the brutal Ashen Citadel (81–100). In the Citadel foes sometimes encircle you with a gap in the ring: follow the gold arrow and run for it. A boss waits on every 5th wave (5, 10, 15…) and a smaller sub-boss on waves 3, 8, 13…. Every wave brings more foes, and they grow tougher every few waves. Kill foes to drop green orbs — they drift to you. Level up to earn skill points, then pause to spend them on Sword, Fireball, Energy Blast, or bonuses. Upgrades never cap.

Touch: left thumb = move · ⚔ sword (swings by itself when foes are in reach on touch, switch that off in Settings; hold it to swing anyway) · 🔥 fireball · ⚡ tap once to start charging (it keeps charging while you fight), tap again to fire, or hold and release. Drag on ⚡ to aim the blast in any direction; with no drag it fires at the nearest foe. Sword and fireball aim at the nearest foe. 💨 Dash (the button, or double-tap the move stick) slips through attacks for a split second.
Keyboard + mouse: WASD/arrows move · J/Space or left-click sword · K or right-click fire · hold L/E (or middle-click) energy · mouse aims · Shift dash · 1/2/3 spend points after a wave · P/Esc pause.
Controller: left stick move · right stick aim · A sword · X fire · hold B or RT energy · Y dash · Start pause · D-pad/A in menus.

Ultimate: every kill fills the purple meter under your XP bar. When it is full, tap the glowing ULT button (key R, or the pad Back button) — Ren awakens as Moon Awakening, Alucard unleashes the Crimson Eclipse.

Perks: every 5 waves you pick 1 of 3 perks that last the whole run.

Gear: foes sometimes drop glowing weapons and pets — walk over them. You can carry 10. They attack on their own; grabbing a duplicate levels it up (max 10). Pause to drop one. Bosses drop two. Sword upgrades also stretch its reach. Arrows at the screen edge point to gear drops and to bosses.

Evolutions: take two matching pieces to Lv 10, then fuse them in the pause menu into one stronger item that also frees a slot: 🪃+☄️ Solar Comet · 🐆+🌩️ Thunder Lynx · ❄️+🪼 Absolute Zero · 🗡️+🩸 Crimson Blades · 🔱+🔔 Worldbreaker · 🐢+🐦‍🔥 Solar Phoenix.

The sword can swing while you charge. Charge longer = bigger and stronger; a full charge fills a quarter of the screen and flies for 10 seconds.


Alucard (rises when you clear wave 25): long thin blade with extra reach (a narrow arc, and it drinks a little blood), ❄️ frost shots that slow foes, a 🩸 blood beam instead of the energy ball (same 3 charge stages) that stays in a straight line, can be steered while it fires (drag ⚡, mouse, or right stick) and can spin all the way around, and a bat-swarm dash. He keeps a separate saved run.
